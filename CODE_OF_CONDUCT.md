# Code of conduct

GitGlasses follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews and any other project space.

In short: be welcoming and considerate, assume good intent, disagree about
ideas rather than people, and accept that the maintainer has the final say on
what the project does.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer,
[@danielwoz](https://github.com/danielwoz), privately using the contact
details on their GitHub profile, rather than raising it in public. Reports are
handled confidentially, and the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant decide the response.
